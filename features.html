Features

A closer look at how Orbit is built around you.

Browsing

A clean home designed around where you want to go.

  • Fast, clean search and address bar
  • Search engine selection (DuckDuckGo, Brave, etc.)
  • Customizable startup behavior

Tabs

Keep your browsing organized without turning tab management into work.

  • Intuitive tab management
  • Quickly close or select tabs

Personalization

Make the browser your own.

  • Light, Dark, and System theme support
  • Custom accent colors

Privacy & Control

Clear, accessible controls for your data.

  • History controls
  • Clear browsing data (cache, cookies, local storage)
  • Site permissions (camera, microphone, location)
