Explore Orbit

A closer look at how Orbit works.

Clean Browsing

Your home screen should reflect where you want to go, not what advertisers want you to click. Orbit offers a completely clutter-free interface focusing entirely on your search and bookmarks.

Effortless Tabs

Keep your browsing organized without turning tab management into a chore. Orbit's tab interface is designed to be visually distinct, allowing you to instantly find the page you left off on.

Deep Personalization

Light mode, Dark mode, or matched to your system. Select your own accent colors and adapt the browser's UI to feel like a native extension of your device.

Granular Control

Search engine freedom, startup behavior, and strict site permissions. You decide exactly which sites have access to your hardware and location, all from a clear settings panel.
