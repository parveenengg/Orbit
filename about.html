About Orbit

The zero-telemetry web browser.

Why Orbit?

Orbit was created to provide a minimal, privacy-focused open-source web browser engineered for pure focus, native speed, zero developer-side tracking, and distraction-free clarity. Browse without the noise.

What We Believe

  • Simplicity: An ultra-minimalist architecture. No crypto wallets. No sponsored wallpapers. No unsolicited AI sidebars.
  • User Control: Search Engine Freedom. Choose your preferred engine with zero forced bias.
  • Privacy: 100% Open Source & Zero Dev-Side Tracking. What you browse stays strictly between you and your device.
  • Performance: Sub-millisecond responsiveness and silent battery conservation.

Built By

Orbit is proudly and independently created by Parveen Kumar. It is engineered with the Obvious Choice philosophy to stay out of your way.

Community

Orbit is an open development project. As the browser evolves, this space will grow to feature the maintainers, contributors, and community members who help make the internet a cleaner place. Learn how to contribute.
