Core Manifesto

The Three Inviolable Tenets

Orbit was not built to be another browser. It was built as a conscious rebellion against modern software entropy.

01 / OPEN SOURCE & PRIVACY

Zero Dev-Side Tracking

Orbit is open source and built with pure respect for privacy. There are zero tracking beacons, zero analytics SDKs, and zero telemetry code from our side. What you browse stays strictly between you and your screen.

02 / PRODUCTIVITY

Fast Form Filling

Tired of repetitive typing on mobile and desktop? Orbit features lightning-fast form filling so you can checkout, log in, and submit forms in one tap—without handing your identity over to big-tech surveillance.

03 / UTILITY

Bookmarks Save & Restore

Cloud conveniences are kept strictly minimal and useful: effortlessly save your favorite sites and restore them cleanly across devices whenever you need. Simple, reliable, and completely free of tracking bloat.
