Minimalism by Architecture.

We stripped away the crypto tokens, the telemetry beacons, and the bloated AI sidebars. What remains is a pure, responsive tool built for human attention.

01 / PRIVACY

Zero Dev-Side Tracking

Orbit is open source. There are no telemetry pings, no analytics beacons, and no tracking code from our side. What you browse stays strictly on your machine.

02 / SPEED

Fast Form Filling

Repetitive data entry belongs in the past. Orbit features instant, intelligent form filling that speeds up logins and checkouts without sending your data to the cloud.

03 / SIMPLICITY

Bookmarks Save & Restore

Cloud conveniences are kept strictly minimal and practical: seamlessly save your essential bookmarks and restore them cleanly across devices whenever you need.
