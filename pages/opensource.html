01 / FIND YOUR WAY IN

You don’t have to
start with a pull request.

A useful question, a reproducible bug report, or a clearer explanation can be a meaningful contribution.

01

Follow the project

Visit Orbit on GitHub. Star the repository to keep it close, or watch it for releases and updates.

↗ 02

Report an issue or edge case

Describe what happened, what you expected, and how to reproduce it. Include your platform and version.

↗ 03

Submit an improvement

Keep changes focused. Explain the reason, show the result, and describe how you checked it.

↗
02 / FROM CURIOSITY TO CONTRIBUTION

Understand it.
Then make it better.

Follow progress on GitHub and share clear, focused feedback as the browser develops.

01 / ORIENT

Start with context.

Check existing issues before reporting a problem. Someone may already be discussing what you noticed.

02 / FOCUS

Choose one change.

A small, complete improvement is easier to discuss and review. For larger ideas, explain the proposal before building it.

03 / VERIFY

Show your work.

Test the behavior you changed. Include screenshots for interface work and reproduction steps for fixes.
