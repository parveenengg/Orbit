01 / INTEGRITY VERIFICATION

Cryptographic Checksum

You do not need to take our word for binary integrity. Every public release is published with a cryptographic SHA-256 hash so you can verify that your download has not been tampered with.

shasum -a 256 downloads/Orbit.apk

Expected SHA-256 (v1.0.1 Android Release):

932e81871003b12ccda0f6c17f5816a1bcaf13482fda6273d187c08dcf748bb4
