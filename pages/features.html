WHAT ORBIT STANDS FOR

Open by choice.
Personal by design.

01 / IN THE OPEN

Follow what comes next.

Orbit is an independent, open-source browser. Follow the project, ask questions, and help shape what comes next.

Follow on GitHub ↗
02 / YOUR DATA

A clear privacy boundary.

Orbit is built around a simple commitment: not selling your browsing data. Websites and search engines have their own data practices; using Orbit does not prevent them from collecting information.

Read about privacy ↗
ON THE HORIZON · NOT AVAILABLE TODAY

More room
to make it yours.

The long term ambition is a browser you can extend with your own features and workflows. Inspired by the freedom of open systems, shaped by the people who use it.

This is a direction for the future, not a feature in the current release. There is no announced release date.
