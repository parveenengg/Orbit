01 / YOUR OPTIONS

Two ways to reach us.

Use whichever is most comfortable for you.

THIS FORM

Private submission

Use the form below to send a report directly. Name and email are optional. Nothing is collected beyond what you type.

  • Feedback, bugs, feature requests, support queries.
  • Optionally attach a screenshot (JPEG/PNG/WebP/GIF, max 5 MB).
  • Optionally share your app version and platform.
  • Email is optional — only needed if you want a reply.
GITHUB ISSUES

Public issue tracker

Orbit's GitHub repository is public. Filing an issue is useful for bug reports you want the community to see and verify.

  • Issues are public — visible to anyone.
  • Requires a GitHub account.
  • Ideal for reproducible bugs or feature discussions.
  • github.com/parveenengg/Orbit/issues

This form stores your submission in Supabase (our backend data provider). We use it to read and respond to reports. Your data is not sold, profiled, or shared with advertisers. See the Privacy Policy for full details.
