01 / KNOW THE DIFFERENCE

A browser is one part
of your privacy.

Orbit, your chosen search engine, and the external websites you visit play distinct roles. Understanding this distinction empowers you to browse intentionally.

WITHIN ORBIT

The browser’s responsibility

Our commitment is absolute zero tracking by the developer. What happens in Orbit stays solely on your local device hardware.

  • Zero telemetry engines, zero analytics beacons, zero crash pings.
  • Bookmarks, history, and passwords remain in local device sandbox.
  • Omnibar keystrokes stay offline; no typing suggestions sent to our servers.
  • Easily clear cache, cookies, and browsing sessions anytime.
BEYOND THE BROWSER

The wider web has its own rules

Search providers and websites you visit can receive the queries and network requests you send. Their cookies and privacy policies apply.

  • Search queries go directly to your chosen engine (DuckDuckGo, Brave, Google).
  • Websites you log into associate activity with your account on that site.
  • Orbit provides controls, but does not claim network-level anonymity (e.g. Tor).

This page explains Orbit’s architecture, engineering boundaries, and formal privacy commitments.

02 / PRACTICAL CONTROL

Meaningful everyday controls.

Everyday browsing features designed to put user agency ahead of corporate extraction.

Choose where your searches go

Select your default search provider with zero corporate bias. Orbit supports DuckDuckGo, Brave Search, Startpage, Google, Bing, or any custom query URL. Orbit never intercepts, proxies, or logs queries.

Review history and granular data deletion

Your browsing history, cached assets, and website cookies are stored in isolated local storage. You can purge them with a single tap or enable automatic session clearing.

Camera, microphone & location permissions

Orbit strictly respects OS-level sandboxing. Sites cannot access your camera, microphone, or geolocation without your explicit runtime authorization.

Audit zero-telemetry in the source code

Because Orbit is 100% open source, our claims are publicly verifiable. You can inspect network sockets, grep for endpoints, and confirm that zero phone-home routines exist.

03 / FORMAL LEGAL POLICY

Orbit Formal Privacy Policy

This formal policy applies to the Orbit application across all operating systems and the official website at orbitbrowser.com.

1. Information Orbit Does NOT Collect

Orbit is engineered from the ground up on a Zero-Telemetry architecture. We do NOT collect, transmit, store, or sell:

• Your browsing history, visited URLs, or page contents.
• Your search engine queries or omnibar keystrokes.
• Unique device identifiers (IMEI, MAC address, advertising ID).
• Geolocation coordinates or IP-to-identity mapping.
• Personal demographic profiles or behavioural cohorts.
• Crash reports containing user data (crash diagnosis is handled locally by OS crash facilities).

2. Local Data Storage on Your Device

The Orbit application stores certain information locally on your device hardware to fulfill core browser operations:

• Bookmarks & Tabs: Saved in the app's local sandbox.
• Cookies & Local Storage: Stored per-domain to keep you logged into the sites you choose.
• Autofill & Form Data: Kept on-device and never synchronized to any cloud server by Orbit.
• Settings & Appearance: Theme preference (dark/light) and search engine choices.

You can wipe this data at any time via Orbit Settings or by uninstalling the application.

3. Orbit Website (orbitbrowser.com)

Our website is hosted on high-performance static hosting (Vercel CDN). When you visit orbitbrowser.com:

• We do not use third-party advertising cookies, Facebook Pixels, or cross-site tracking tags.
• Standard ephemeral web server access logs (IP address, user agent, timestamp) are automatically recorded by CDN infrastructure solely for DDoS mitigation, infrastructure health, and network security.
• We respect your browser's Do Not Track (DNT) and Global Privacy Control (GPC) headers.

4. Third-Party Search Engines & External Links

When you submit a search query in Orbit, the query is transmitted over HTTPS directly to the search provider you have configured (e.g., DuckDuckGo, Brave Search, or Google). That communication is governed by the chosen search provider's own privacy policy. Orbit injects no tracking parameters, affiliate tags, or referral codes into your searches.

5. GDPR & CCPA / CPRA Compliance

Because Orbit collects zero personal data, we do not sell or share personal data under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) or European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Under GDPR Articles 15–22, users have rights regarding personal data. Since Orbit holds no server-side user databases, accounts, or telemetry, the data controller has no records to produce, correct, or delete—all your data resides exclusively under your direct custody on your device.

6. Children's Privacy

Orbit does not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). The software is a general-purpose, privacy-preserving browsing tool.
