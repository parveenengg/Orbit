ABOUT / AN INDEPENDENT ORBIT

A little morespace to browse.

Orbit is a personal, open-source browser project by Parveen Kumar. An exploration of what everyday browsing can feel like when clarity and user control come first.

Explore the browser ↗ Meet the maker ↓
Independent product Open-source approach Designed around the user
01 / THE STARTING POINT

The browser is where so much of our day happens. Orbit explores a simple idea: give that space back to the person using it.

A clear interface, room for personal preferences, and a commitment not to build a business around selling browsing data.

02 / THE DESIGN COMPASS

Less friction.
More intention.

01

Clarity comes first.

Make the next action easy to understand. Give screens, typography, and motion a purpose, with space for the content that matters.

02

Leave room for choice.

Search preferences, appearance, and browsing settings should feel like personal decisions. Orbit aims to make those choices approachable.

03

Build where people can look.

Open-source development makes the work available to examine and discuss. Questions and feedback are part of improving the product.

03 / THE PERSON BEHIND ORBIT

Built by
Parveen Kumar.

Orbit brings product thinking, interface design, and engineering together in one independent project. It is a place to turn ideas into working software, learn from real use, and refine the details.
