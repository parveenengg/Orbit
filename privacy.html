01 / KNOW THE DIFFERENCE

A browser is one part
of your privacy.

Orbit, your search engine, and the sites you visit play different roles. Understanding that distinction helps you make better choices.

WITHIN ORBIT

The browser’s responsibility.

The project’s commitment is to avoid tracking by the developer and the sale of browsing data. Follow the project on GitHub to keep up with its development and raise questions.

  • Choose your search engine.
  • Review browsing settings and history.
  • Use the available controls to clear browsing data.
BEYOND THE BROWSER

The wider web has its own rules.

Search providers and websites can receive the requests you send, along with information you share or make available to them. Their accounts, cookies, and privacy policies still matter.

  • A search query goes to the provider you choose.
  • Signing in connects activity to that service.
  • Orbit does not promise anonymity across the web.

This page explains Orbit’s product principles and their boundaries. It is not a live network audit or a guarantee about external services.

02 / PRACTICAL CONTROL

Small choices.
Meaningful control.

Start with what you can review and change in your everyday browsing.

Choose where your searches go

Select a provider that suits you and review its privacy settings. Changing browsers does not change a search provider’s own data practices.

Review your history and browsing data

Use Orbit’s browsing settings to review history and clear available categories of browsing data. Clearing local data does not erase information a website or search provider has already received.

Pay attention to site permissions

Review permission requests in context. Only grant access when a website needs it for something you want to do; available controls can differ by platform.

Follow, question, and contribute

GitHub is a place to follow progress and raise questions about the browser. If you identify a privacy concern, provide clear reproduction steps without posting private browsing information or credentials.
