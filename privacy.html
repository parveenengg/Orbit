Privacy

Factual transparency regarding your data.

What stays on your device

Your browsing history, site data, cache, and local storage stay entirely on your device. Orbit does not sync or transmit your browsing activity to any external server or corporate cloud.

Telemetry & Analytics

Orbit does not use developer-side telemetry. We do not track how many tabs you open, which settings you change, or how long you use the browser. We rely on community feedback rather than silent background analytics.

Site Permissions

You have full, granular control over site permissions. Any website requesting access to your camera, microphone, or location must be explicitly approved by you. You can revoke these permissions at any time through the Settings interface.

Search Engines

By default, Orbit allows you to choose your preferred search engine (such as DuckDuckGo, Brave Search, Startpage, Google, or Bing). When you search, your queries are subject to the privacy policy of the search engine you have chosen.

Data Deletion

You can instantly wipe your browsing history, cookies, cache, and site data through the "Clear Browsing Data" option in Settings. Once deleted, this data cannot be recovered.
