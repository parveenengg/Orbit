The Orbit Manifesto

The Internet Forgot
How to Be Quiet.

Somewhere in the last decade, web browsers stopped being neutral instruments for human discovery and became surveillance outposts for advertising conglomerates.

Chrome quietly became an ad-broker’s operating system—consuming multiple gigabytes of memory to track your behavioral profile across tabs. Brave attempted to fix privacy, but cluttered your screen with crypto wallets, token rewards, and sponsored wallpapers. Other experimental browsers introduced complexity with steep learning curves and heavy memory overhead.

“People don’t need another browser that tries to manage their life. They need a window that gets out of their way.”

Orbit was engineered to strip away the circus. We built an open-source browser with zero developer-side tracking. You pick whatever search engine you want. You fill out forms fast. You save your bookmarks cleanly. And when you open a page, your computer's memory and battery are treated with respect.

No crypto. No bloatware. Just pure, serene web speed.

Open Source Transparency

Orbit’s source code is public. You don’t have to take our word for it—audit the repository, inspect the network stack, or contribute.
